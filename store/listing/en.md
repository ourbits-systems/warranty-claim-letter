## Detailed description

Something you bought stopped working? Warranty Claim Assistant helps you write a clear, professional letter to the seller, right in your browser's side panel.

WHAT IT WRITES
• Warranty claims
• Repair, replacement and refund requests
• Follow-ups when the seller does not reply
• Responses when the seller rejects your claim
• Formal complaints to a consumer organisation

HOW IT WORKS
1. Click the toolbar icon to open the assistant next to the page you are on.
2. Choose what you need to write, then enter the product and describe the problem.
3. Choose the country where you bought it. The assistant checks whether the legal guarantee may still apply.
4. Edit the letter, then copy it, open it in your email app, or save it as a PDF.

COUNTRIES AND LANGUAGES
• Consumer rules for Germany, France, the Netherlands, Spain, Ireland and other EU countries
• Letters in English, or in German, French, Spanish or Dutch for those countries
• Interface in English, German, French, Spanish, Hindi and Arabic

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account needed
• Everything you type stays in your browser. Nothing is sent to HoldMyBill or anyone else.
• The extension cannot see the pages you visit
• Delete your drafts at any time

NOT LEGAL ADVICE
The assistant describes your rights in general terms. It does not cite laws for a country until a lawyer has checked that country's rules. Read the letter before you send it.
